{{- $domain := .Values.global.bbe.domain }}
Music Assistant plays the music at https://music.{{ $domain }}, on speakers it finds on the LAN, in the browser and in
its apps (connect them to music.{{ $domain }}, port 443). Its admin is {{ .Values.bbe.admin.username }}, with the password:
  kubectl get secret bbe-music-admin -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} -o jsonpath='{.data.password}' | base64 -d
It can't sign people in with authentik yet, so add people in its Settings → Users. To play Spotify too, add the
Spotify provider in its Settings → Providers and sign in with your Premium account. It plays a local copy of a track
when there is one, also when Spotify is down.

Lidarr finds albums at https://lidarr.{{ $domain }}, slskd shows Soulseek at https://slskd.{{ $domain }}, and SoulSync
mirrors Spotify at https://soulsync.{{ $domain }}, all for authentik's admins. slskd asks for its own login after
authentik's, which is:
  kubectl get secret bbe-music-slskd -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} -o jsonpath='{.data.username}' | base64 -d
  kubectl get secret bbe-music-slskd -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} -o jsonpath='{.data.password}' | base64 -d

{{- if .Values.global.bbe.vpn.enabled }}
All of slskd's traffic goes through the VPN, and Soulseek peers reach it through the port the VPN forwards. With the
tunnel down, slskd leaves Soulseek and nothing but the cluster is reachable.
{{- else if .Values.bbe.music.slskd.loadBalancerIP }}
slskd reaches Soulseek directly, and peers reach it at {{ .Values.bbe.music.slskd.loadBalancerIP }}, port {{ .Values.bbe.music.slskd.listenPort }}. Forward that TCP port
on your router to it, or slskd only reaches peers whose own port is open.
{{- else }}
slskd reaches Soulseek directly, and no port is forwarded to it, so it only reaches peers whose own port is open. Set
bbe.music.slskd.loadBalancerIP and forward its port on your router, or turn on the VPN, for the others.
{{- end }}
Soulseek's rules don't allow automated clients like Soularr, and expect you to share: slskd shares the music folder,
at most {{ .Values.bbe.music.slskd.uploadSlots }} uploads at once.

To mirror your Spotify library with SoulSync, once:
  1. Create an app at https://developer.spotify.com/dashboard with your Premium account, with the redirect URI
     https://soulsync.{{ $domain }}/callback
  2. Put its client ID and secret in SoulSync's Settings → Spotify, and sign in there. Spotify asks again every 6 months.
  3. Choose the playlists to mirror, and your Liked Songs. Spotify only lets SoulSync read playlists you own or
     collaborate on, so save a copy of others' as your own first.
SoulSync writes them into music/ with Lidarr's naming, skipping what's already there, and an M3U per playlist in
music/Playlists/ that Music Assistant reads.
