{{- if .Values.bbe.media.ingress.enabled }}
The apps are at https://<app>.{{ .Values.global.bbe.domain }}: sonarr, radarr, prowlarr, bazarr, qbittorrent, jellyfin and seerr.
{{- end }}
{{- if .Values.global.bbe.sso.enabled }}
People sign in with their authentik account. Sonarr, Radarr, Prowlarr, Bazarr and qBittorrent let in its admins.
Jellyfin lets in everyone in `users` and `admins`, with the same password in its TV and phone apps, and makes admins
its administrators. Seerr signs people in through Jellyfin.

bbe's own login stays, for when authentik can't sign anyone in. Jellyfin and Seerr accept it, and every app has it:
{{- else }}

Every app's web UI uses the same login:
{{- end }}
  username: {{ .Values.bbe.admin.username }}
  password: kubectl get secret bbe-media-admin -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} -o jsonpath='{.data.password}' | base64 -d
{{- if .Values.bbe.setup.enabled }}

The apps are being connected to each other in the background, which can take a few minutes on a
first install. To follow along:
  kubectl logs -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} -f job/bbe-media-setup-{{ .Release.Revision }}
{{- end }}

Add indexers with `bbe configure {{ .Release.Name }}`, and Prowlarr shares them with Sonarr and Radarr.
Seerr signs in with the same login, through Jellyfin.
{{- if .Values.global.bbe.vpn.enabled }}

All of qBittorrent's traffic goes through the VPN ({{ .Values.global.bbe.vpn.provider }}), and peers reach it through the port the VPN
forwards. With the tunnel down, nothing but the cluster is reachable. To see the tunnel:
  kubectl logs -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} deploy/{{ .Release.Name }}-qbittorrent -c gluetun
{{- end }}
