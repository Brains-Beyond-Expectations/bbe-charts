The books are at https://calibre.{{ .Values.global.bbe.domain }}, and Shelfmark finds more at
https://shelfmark.{{ .Values.global.bbe.domain }}.

People in authentik's `users` and `admins` groups sign in to both with authentik. The library lets in whoever is
signed in to authentik, and creates them on their first visit. In the library, `admins` are its admins, from bbe's
setup on every install and upgrade. New people may download, read and keep shelves.

E-readers and their apps sign in with their authentik username and password:
  OPDS feed:   https://calibre.{{ .Values.global.bbe.domain }}/opds
  Kobo sync:   each person makes their Kobo link in their profile at https://calibre.{{ .Values.global.bbe.domain }}/me

Books Shelfmark downloads, and books dropped in `books-ingest/` on the share, are added to the library and removed from
`books-ingest/` by themselves. The library itself is in `books/`.

The library's own admin, for when authentik can't help, is {{ .Values.bbe.admin.username }}. The library is behind
authentik, so it signs in at http://localhost:8083 after:
  kubectl port-forward -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} svc/{{ .Release.Name }}-calibre 8083:8083
Its password:
  kubectl get secret bbe-library-admin -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} -o jsonpath='{.data.password}' | base64 -d
