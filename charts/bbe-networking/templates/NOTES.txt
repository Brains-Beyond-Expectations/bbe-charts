Apps are served at https://<app>.{{ .Values.global.bbe.domain }}, through the ingress at {{ .Values.global.bbe.ingress.loadBalancerIP }}.

Blocky answers for {{ .Values.global.bbe.domain }} and everything under it, so devices find the apps once they use
Blocky as their DNS server, which is easiest to set in your router's DHCP settings:
  kubectl get service {{ .Release.Name }}-blocky -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} -o jsonpath='{.status.loadBalancer.ingress[0].ip}'

The apps' certificate is signed by bbe's own certificate authority. Browsers and apps warn about it until a device
trusts it. To save it, then add it to the device's trusted certificates:
  kubectl get secret bbe-ca -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} -o jsonpath='{.data.ca\.crt}' | base64 -d > bbe-ca.crt
