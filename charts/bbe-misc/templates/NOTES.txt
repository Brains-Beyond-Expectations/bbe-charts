Snipe-IT is at https://snipeit.{{ .Values.global.bbe.domain }}.

People in authentik's `users` and `admins` groups are signed in with their authentik session. Snipe-IT brings them in
from authentik's LDAP on the schedule `{{ .Values.bbe.setup.peopleSchedule }}`. Until then, they sign in on its login
form with their authentik password. `users` see what's checked out to them. `admins` can do everything.

Snipe-IT's own admin, {{ .Values.bbe.misc.snipeit.admin.username }}, isn't in authentik. Its password:
  kubectl get secret bbe-misc-admin -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} -o jsonpath='{.data.password}' | base64 -d
